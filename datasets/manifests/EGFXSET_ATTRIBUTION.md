# EGFxSet attribution

The M1 real-DI ingestion check uses `Clean/Neck/6-22.wav` from **EGFxSet: Electric guitar tones processed through real effects of distortion, modulation, delay and reverb**, version 1.0.

Creators: Hegel Pedroza, Gerardo Meza, and Iran R. Roman. Source: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7044411. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0).

The raw archive and selected WAV remain under `datasets/raw/` and are ignored by Git. The repository stores only source metadata, cryptographic checksums required by the campaign protocol, and derived numerical summaries.
